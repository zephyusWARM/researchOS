## Remaining-corpus triage
High-priority signals: noodle/rice shop, dumplings, pizza-by-slice, casual pizza, skewers, café/teahouse, casual Taiwanese/Hakka, obvious solo mains. Low-priority rapid exclusions: tasting-menu, omakase, high-end sushi/kappo, hotel fine dining, course-only teppanyaki.

Do not exclude a shared Chinese/Taiwanese restaurant merely because many dishes exceed 500: first look for a genuine solo fried-rice/noodle/set path. Conversely, never qualify by dividing a multi-person bill.

## Stable-survivor requirements
Current menu; all mandatory charges; concrete normal solo order <=500; portion sufficiency; solo ordering; hours/open status; queue/reservation/sell-out friction; payment friction if material; recent positive and negative evidence; award-dish identity/price/availability when discoverable; falsification condition.

## Stop condition
The first full-corpus milestone is complete only when all 314 have at least a defensible triage disposition and every QUALIFIED/BORDERLINE survivor has current attributable price evidence.
