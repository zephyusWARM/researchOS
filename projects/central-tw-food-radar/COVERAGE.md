## Historical backfill lane

Phase 2 must inspect 2023–2025 500碗 and previous MICHELIN editions. Historical recognition does **not** require a restaurant to appear in the latest 2026 list to remain interesting; it is used as a persistence signal.

Example high-information historical lead:
- 2025 500碗 awarded **3 bowls** to 彰化的北斗肉圓詹 and 北門口肉圓（總店／民生店）. These are not automatically promoted into the current-2026 corpus; they enter the historical-recurrence lane.

## Verification fields for every candidate

- canonical entity / aliases / branch
- city / district / precise address
- award source + year + level
- awarded or signature dish
- current menu availability
- realistic solo spend
- current hours / closed days
- queue / sell-out / reservation friction
- cash/card/order method
- current positive evidence
- current negative evidence
- last verified date
- falsification condition
- research disposition: UNVERIFIED / ACTIVE / LOGISTICS-RISK / STALE / CLOSED / NOT-WORTH-TRIP / TRIP-CANDIDATE

## Stop condition for Phase 1

The current census is complete when all latest-2026 candidates are entity-resolved and every high-information candidate has a current operating-status + dish + hours verification. No trip-ready shortlist should be considered stable before the negative-evidence pass.
