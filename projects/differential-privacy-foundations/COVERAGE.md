# Coverage

The project remains incomplete until it covers:

- neighboring-dataset semantics and threat model
- pure and approximate DP
- post-processing and group privacy
- global/local/smooth sensitivity
- randomized response, Laplace, Gaussian, and exponential mechanisms
- basic/advanced composition and amplification
- central, local, and shuffle models
- RDP, zCDP, f-DP/GDP, privacy-loss distributions/profiles, and conversions
- utility/sample complexity and lower bounds
- DP-SGD: clipping, noise, sampling assumptions, accounting, tuning, and reporting
- auditing, implementation pitfalls, randomness/numerics, and deployment
- statistical/hypothesis-testing interpretations

## Adversarial checks

Challenge whether:
1. a non-technical primer is sufficient for rigorous mastery;
2. Dwork–Roth (2014) is too old to remain the best foundations spine;
3. a modern ML guide should replace rather than supplement foundations;
4. knowing the definition without being able to derive mechanisms/composition constitutes understanding.

## Stop condition

Stop only when the source hierarchy, conceptual dependency graph, mastery tests, and modern-extension map remain stable under further search and P0 uncertainty is bounded.
