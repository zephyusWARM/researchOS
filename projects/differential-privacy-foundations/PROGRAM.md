# Program — Differential Privacy Foundations

## Mission

Build a durable, source-backed route from zero background to the ability to read, derive, criticize, and apply differential privacy (DP), including modern differentially private machine learning.

The program is not a bibliography contest. It distinguishes conceptual primers, mathematically substantive articles, comprehensive foundations, and modern ML/deployment bridges.

## Core questions

- Which single source best covers the foundational dependency graph of DP?
- If the format must literally be one article, which paper is the strongest entry?
- How do adjacency, the DP inequality, sensitivity, mechanisms, post-processing, group privacy, composition, and utility fit together causally?
- When do approximate DP, Gaussian mechanisms, amplification, RDP, zCDP, f-DP, and privacy accounting become necessary?
- How does the theory connect to DP-SGD, clipping, sampling, accounting, tuning, auditing, and reporting?
- Which historical results are foundational but poor first teaching sources?

## Source policy

Prefer primary papers, official author/publisher copies, established community resources, and high-quality reviews. Citation counts and search snippets are discovery aids, not evidence.

## Persistence policy

Research first; batch-persist meaningful epistemic deltas. Keep source fact, comparative inference, and unresolved questions distinct.
