## Highest-priority gaps

1. Direct human autophagic-flux measurements and tissue-specific biopsy evidence.
2. Controlled comparisons that isolate fasting duration from energy intake and weight loss.
3. Dose-response evidence spanning approximately 12–36 hours.
4. Long-term clinical outcomes attributable to fasting duration itself rather than correlated lifestyle changes.
5. Counterexamples or populations in which longer fasting may have distinct effects.
