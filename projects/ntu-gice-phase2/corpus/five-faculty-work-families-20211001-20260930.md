## Counting rules
- Unit of count is a work family, not a webpage hit.
- arXiv/preprint -> formal conference/journal is one family.
- conference -> journal with the same title/content is one lineage and is not used to inflate topical density.
- Student theses are lineage evidence, not publication-count evidence.
- Same-name hits are excluded unless affiliation/coauthor/ORCID/topic continuity resolves identity.
- Workshop papers are retained, but are labeled separately from archival conference/journal papers.
- Date boundary follows formal publication/venue date when available. An early-access item before 2021-10-01 whose issue publication falls inside the window is retained and flagged.

## Cross-faculty red-team findings
1. Official faculty publication pages can be stale (Chou) or contain misleading surname-only records (Yeh/Benson Yeh). They are seed sources, not ground truth.
2. DBLP/OpenAlex are useful for versioning and identity, but name collisions remain material (especially Tzu-Yu Liu and Pei-Yuan Wu).
3. Last-author position is not automatically corresponding authorship. Explicit corresponding-author metadata was used when available.
4. For young/new labs (Liu), research-program evidence can lead publication evidence by 1-3 years; classification confidence must reflect that lag.
