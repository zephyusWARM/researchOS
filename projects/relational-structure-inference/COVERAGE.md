# Coverage

The search space is considered incomplete until the project has examined, at minimum:

- direct NRI / latent edge-type inference family
- precursors before 2018 that materially shaped the formulation
- dynamic or time-varying relation inference
- continuous-time / Neural ODE relational dynamics
- temporal graph structure learning
- causal discovery and identifiability overlap
- object-centric relational dynamics
- physics-informed and equivariant interaction learning
- higher-order and hypergraph interaction inference
- multi-agent / social-behavior relation inference
- benchmark design and structure-recovery evaluation validity
- undersampling, observability, identifiability, and confounding failure modes
- primary-source citation/reference chains across 2016–2026

## Adversarial checks

At least one deliberate red-team pass must challenge:

1. the claim that NRI is the central ancestor of the selected line;
2. the selected "important papers";
3. any reading order based mainly on recency or citation count;
4. any inference that good trajectory prediction means the latent graph is correct.

## Stop condition

Stop only when new searches no longer materially change the genealogy, high-value paper set, major assumptions/failure modes, or reading-path decision, and remaining P0 uncertainty is either resolved or explicitly bounded.
