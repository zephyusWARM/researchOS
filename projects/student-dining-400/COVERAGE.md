## Adversarial checks

1. Do not equate a cheap single dish with a qualifying meal.
2. Search specifically for low-spend, water, service, set-menu, and sharing constraints.
3. For every survivor, seek a recent negative/critical source, not only praise.
4. Separate the 500盤 award dish from what is actually on sale now.
5. Treat an old price as stale even when the restaurant itself is unchanged.
6. If two current sources disagree on exact price but both exceed NT$400 by a large margin, preserve the disagreement while keeping the exclusion.

## Required survivor depth

Before a survivor is considered stable, capture current menu/price, mandatory charges, portion sufficiency, current hours, queue/sell-out risk, payment/ordering friction, recent positive evidence, recent negative evidence, and a falsification condition.

## Stop condition

Phase 1 is complete when every seed restaurant has a defensible budget disposition and every active survivor has a current first-party or restaurant-controlled menu check plus an adversarial quality/operational pass. Ongoing tracking then refreshes only active/borderline restaurants or excluded restaurants with evidence of a material price-policy change.
