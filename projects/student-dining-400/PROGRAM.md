# Program — Student Dining <= NT$400

## Mission

Find restaurants that are unusually good or interesting **without breaking a student's hard NT$400 per-person budget**. The research target is not the cheapest visible item; it is a realistic one-person meal whose unavoidable bill remains at or below NT$400.

Phase 1 screens the ten Taipei restaurants selected by James C. Lin in the 2026-09-21 500盤 feature. Restaurants that fail the ceiling are retained only as exclusion evidence; the active watchlist follows survivors.

## Hard budget rule

A restaurant qualifies only when current evidence supports a realistic one-person meal with an unavoidable bill <= NT$400.

Include mandatory minimum spend, service charge, required set menu, cover/seat charge, required drink/water charge, and tax if not included. Do not use one-off coupons or delivery-only discounts as the baseline. A sub-NT$400 appetizer, dessert, or share plate does not qualify by itself if it is not a normal one-person meal.

## Research questions

- Which seed restaurants have a defensible <=NT$400 solo-meal path today?
- Is the affordable path permanent, seasonal, limited, or promotion-dependent?
- Are there hidden minimums or service charges that invalidate a seemingly cheap dish?
- Is the portion a real meal for one person?
- How volatile are menu price, availability, queue, and sell-out risk?
- What recent negative evidence could overturn the recommendation?
- What price/policy change would remove a restaurant from the watchlist?

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Official menu, booking page, or restaurant-controlled announcement.
2. Current restaurant-managed reservation/menu platform.
3. Current delivery menu as price corroboration only.
4. Current structured business/menu data.
5. Recent independent dining reports with visit date and photographed/menu-backed prices.
6. Old menus/reviews only as historical evidence.

Search snippets are discovery aids, not standalone evidence unless the underlying attributable page is captured as a Source.

## Persistence policy

GitHub is durable truth, not a live notebook. Batch only meaningful Source→Evidence→Claim changes. Preserve contradictory prices and freshness gaps rather than silently choosing one. Revalidate volatile menu evidence before a concrete visit recommendation.
